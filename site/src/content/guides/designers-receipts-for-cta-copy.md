# Designer's Receipts for CTA copy

How BluePainter checks CTA copy against team policy and writes the fix back to TSX.

## What this receipt checks

The **CTA copy** receipt checks button labels against a list of weak, generic words. The policy is flags labels such as “Submit”, “Click here” and “OK”, and your team can change it.

## How the fix works

One click suggests a specific, outcome-led label you can accept or edit, and the change is written back to the TSX as a small diff.

## Why it matters

Generation tools invent UI. Receipts enforce the rules your team already agreed on, so components pass design-system review the first time.
