# Designer's Receipts for feature clutter

How BluePainter checks feature clutter against team policy and writes the fix back to TSX.

## What this receipt checks

The **feature clutter** receipt checks how many feature bullets a card or section carries. The policy is a maximum of five by default, and your team can change it.

## How the fix works

One click flags the overflow so you can trim or regroup the list, and the change is written back to the TSX as a small diff.

## Why it matters

Generation tools invent UI. Receipts enforce the rules your team already agreed on, so components pass design-system review the first time.
