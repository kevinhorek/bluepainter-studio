# Plasmic vs BluePainter

Plasmic focuses on visually building pages and components for React apps. BluePainter keeps canvas and TSX in sync inside your repo and checks design policy with Designer's Receipts.

## Short answer

**Plasmic** focuses on visually building pages and components for React apps. **BluePainter** focuses on AST-preserving canvas ↔ TSX sync plus team policy checks, inside the repo you already have. Many teams will use both.

## Comparison lens

| Lens | What to ask of Plasmic | BluePainter |
|------|------------------------|-------------|
| Starting point | Does it start from a design, a prompt, or your code? | Your existing TSX files |
| Editing existing code | Does a visual edit keep your comments and formatting? | Core — one-line diffs |
| Design policy | Can it enforce your contrast, spacing and copy rules? | Core — Designer's Receipts |
| Where the result lives | Its platform, or your repo? | Your repo |

This page describes positioning, not a feature-by-feature benchmark — check Plasmic's own documentation for its current capabilities.
