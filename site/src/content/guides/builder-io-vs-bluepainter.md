# Builder.io vs BluePainter

Builder.io focuses on visual page building on top of an existing site. BluePainter keeps canvas and TSX in sync inside your repo and checks design policy with Designer's Receipts.

## Short answer

**Builder.io** focuses on visual page building on top of an existing site. **BluePainter** focuses on AST-preserving canvas ↔ TSX sync plus team policy checks, inside the repo you already have. Many teams will use both.

## Comparison lens

| Lens | What to ask of Builder.io | BluePainter |
|------|------------------------|-------------|
| Starting point | Does it start from a design, a prompt, or your code? | Your existing TSX files |
| Editing existing code | Does a visual edit keep your comments and formatting? | Core — one-line diffs |
| Design policy | Can it enforce your contrast, spacing and copy rules? | Core — Designer's Receipts |
| Where the result lives | Its platform, or your repo? | Your repo |

This page describes positioning, not a feature-by-feature benchmark — check Builder.io's own documentation for its current capabilities.
