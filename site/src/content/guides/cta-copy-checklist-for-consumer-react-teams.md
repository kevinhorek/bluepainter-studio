# CTA copy checklist for consumer-app React teams

A short CTA copy checklist for consumer-app teams shipping React, with the Designer's Receipts rule that automates it.

## Why this matters for consumer-app teams

Consumer-app teams iterate on conversion copy and button treatment constantly. CTA copy is one of the first things to drift.

## Checklist

- Primary buttons say what happens next
- Generic labels (“Submit”, “Send”) are replaced
- The weak-word list reflects your own voice guidelines

## Automate it

BluePainter's CTA copy receipt checks button labels against a list of weak, generic words (flags labels such as “Submit”, “Click here” and “OK”). One click suggests a specific, outcome-led label you can accept or edit and writes the change back to your TSX.
