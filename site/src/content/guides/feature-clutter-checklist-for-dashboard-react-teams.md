# Feature clutter checklist for dashboard React teams

A short feature clutter checklist for dashboard teams shipping React, with the Designer's Receipts rule that automates it.

## Why this matters for dashboard teams

Dashboard teams pack tables, filters and cards into one view and fight visual clutter daily. Feature clutter is one of the first things to drift.

## Checklist

- Pricing and feature cards stay at five bullets or fewer
- Longer lists are grouped or moved behind a “compare” link
- The limit in policy matches what design review actually enforces

## Automate it

BluePainter's feature clutter receipt checks how many feature bullets a card or section carries (a maximum of five by default). One click flags the overflow so you can trim or regroup the list and writes the change back to your TSX.
